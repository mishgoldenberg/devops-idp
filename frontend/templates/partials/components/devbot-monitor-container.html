Why questions fail

Questions that ended in an error, by what went wrong
Rate limit and budget mean a person's AI key ran out for the minute or for good: raise the key's limits with whoever issues them. Key refused means an expired or revoked key. Too long for the model means the conversation outgrew it: people should start a new one. AI service error or unreachable is the model service's side. Hub error is ours: its details are on the Logs page.

Models

Questions per model, with how many failed and the typical wait
Which models people pick, and how each one does. A model with many failures or a long typical wait is a reason to change DEVBOT_DEFAULT_MODEL, which is what a person's picker starts on.
