Appearance

{# A three-way select, not a dark-mode toggle. "System" is a third option, and a two-state switch cannot express it: with the preference set to System the toggle had to show either "light" or "dark", one of which is a lie about what is stored. #}

Theme

Saved to your account and applied on every device. "System" follows this computer's own light/dark setting.

Display mode

Comfortable gives widgets more breathing room. Compact fits more on screen.

Reduce motion

Turns off sliding and fading — the sidebar, panels and widget rearranging all snap instead. Worth turning on if this machine has no graphics acceleration, where those animations are drawn by the processor and feel slow. Loading spinners keep spinning.

Streak flame

The small flame beside the bell: how many workdays in a row you have done something in the Hub. Only you and the admins see it.

Auto-refresh

How often widgets quietly re-fetch in the background. They never refresh while you are using them, and the Refresh button always works regardless of this setting.

Keyboard

Shortcuts

The portal has shortcuts for search, navigation and refresh. Press ? anywhere to see them.

Reset layout

Put every widget back to its original position and size. Which widgets you have stays as it is — this only undoes dragging and resizing.
