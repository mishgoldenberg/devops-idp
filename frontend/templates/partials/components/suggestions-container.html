{# HEBREW / RTL. dir="auto" on every element that holds text a USER typed. It is not a global switch and it must not be: the portal's own chrome is English and left-to-right, and flipping the whole app would wreck it. What was actually broken is narrower and worse — a Hebrew sentence rendered inside a left-to-right box has its punctuation thrown to the wrong end and reads backwards. dir="auto" makes the BROWSER decide, per string, from the first strong directional character in it. A Hebrew title aligns right and reads right; the English one under it aligns left. Both are correct, in the same list, with no language setting to configure and nothing for anyone to get wrong. #}
