{# Three things were wrong with this field, and they had one cause: it was declared `type="email"`. 1. The browser treated it as a login-style e-mail box and offered every address it had ever saved — including personal ones typed on a different network. `autocomplete="off"` plus a name the heuristics do not recognise as a contact field stops the suggestion list; the value below is the only thing that should be proposed here. 2. Azure DevOps here is backed by Active Directory, where a principal is often DOMAIN\user rather than an address. `type="email"` REFUSED to submit that form — the browser's own validation rejected a perfectly valid administrator before the request was ever made. 3. It started blank, so the common case (yourself) had to be typed out. `type="text"` is correct: the backend has always accepted any non-empty principal and does the real existence check against Azure DevOps. #} {# Prefill comes from the backend (`ado_admin_default`): DOMAIN\ when a NetBIOS domain is configured, otherwise the bare username. That is the identity form this AD-backed Azure DevOps resolves — a lookup by anything else silently finds no one, which is why the chosen admin was never actually granted access. The field stays editable: a full e-mail or another account are also accepted, and the backend resolves whatever is entered. #}
