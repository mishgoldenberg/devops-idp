{# Logs. The page used to be a flat table of about ten kinds of event — the ones somebody had remembered to call audit.log() for — with no severity, no search, and no way to tell a routine action from a failure. You could not answer "what broke" with it, which is the only question anyone opens a log to answer. It now shows everything the portal does (see backend/app/request_audit.py), and it is built around severity: the levels are chips across the top with live counts, so "ERROR 3" is visible before you go looking for it. The colours are raw CSS rather than Tailwind utilities. `bg-error/10` and friends are not in the compiled stylesheet and would silently do nothing; the DaisyUI theme variables (--er, --wa, --in, --su, --bc) are always there and follow the theme. #}
