{# The self-service form. One renderer for every request the portal offers. It is handed a spec from /api/catalog/forms/ — sections, fields, types, options — and builds the form from it, so adding a request is a dozen lines in catalog_forms.py rather than another hand-built wizard. WHY IT IS NOT A WIZARD The ticket flow is four steps behind a Next button, and so is the ServiceNow form it replaces. Steps are the wrong shape for these requests. You cannot see how much is left to do, you cannot go back to check something without losing your place, a single mistake in step 2 is only discovered at step 4, and the summary at the end is a second rendering of everything you just typed. All of that is scaffolding around one small form. This is one page: grouped sections you can see the whole of, a rail on the left that says which groups are done, and a footer that is always visible and says exactly what happens when you press the button. Validation is on the field you just left, not at the end. Nothing here is a Tailwind class that would need compiling — output.css ships precompiled — so the layout is hand-written CSS scoped to .cf- names. #} {# The name a ticket will carry, for the read-only "Full name" prefill: the identity provider's name, not the editable display name (identity.trusted_name). Rendered by the server because the form itself is built in the browser and has nothing else to read it from. #}
