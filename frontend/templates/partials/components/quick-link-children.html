{# The multi-link children editor — the little "name + link" table a group is made of. It exists as its own partial because there are now TWO forms that edit a quick link: Platform Managing, for the shared list an admin curates, and the dashboard widget, for the personal list only you can see. The markup of the two tables is trivial; the BEHAVIOUR is not — a blank trailing row is normal rather than an error, removing the last row has to leave one behind, and read/write have to agree about which of those rows count. Two copies of that is two copies that eventually disagree, and the symptom would be a group that silently saves fewer links than it shows. So: one implementation, on `window.portalQuickLinkChildren`, taking the it should operate on. Callers keep their own markup and their own ids. Any tbody marked `data-kid-body` gets the remove button wired automatically — there is nothing to bind per form. #}
