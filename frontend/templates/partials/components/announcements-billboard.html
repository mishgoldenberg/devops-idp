{# The billboard: what the people who run the portal want to tell the people who use it. Sits above everything else on the dashboard, because an announcement below the fold is a memo in a drawer. It renders NOTHING when there is nothing to say — no empty card, no heading, no reserved space. A permanent "Announcements" section that is usually empty teaches everyone to skip the top of the page, which is the one thing this must not do. Dismissal is per person and per VERSION: waving one away records the wording it had at the time, so an edited announcement returns for everybody. See backend/app/api/announcements.py. #} {# The .ann-card rules themselves live in static/css/theme.css: the admin editor renders the same markup as a live preview on another page, and one definition is the only way the preview and the real thing stay identical. #}
