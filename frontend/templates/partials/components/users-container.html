First sign-in
When the account was created, which is the first time this person signed in to the Hub.
Last active
When the Hub last saw them using it. "Here now" means within the last half hour. For anyone not measured since tracking began, it is their last sign-in.
Time · 7d / 30d
Their active time in the last 7 and 30 days, counted the same way as "Time in the Hub" above.
Days · 30d
On how many of the last 30 days they used the Hub at all. 20 means almost every working day; 1 means once this month.
Sessions · 30d
How many separate sittings they had in the last 30 days. A sitting ends after 30 minutes with nothing happening.
Streak
Workdays in a row (Sunday to Thursday) on which they used the Hub, worked on a pull request, had a pipeline succeed or moved a work item; their longest run is in brackets. Five freezes a month cover holidays. They see it as the flame beside the bell; only they and admins can.
The arrow
Opens that person's day-by-day activity, the pages they use most, their recent sittings, the systems they have connected and what they did last.

Active time counts only while a Hub tab is on screen and someone has used the mouse or keyboard in the last two minutes, and never more than the clock allows however many tabs are open.
